# Exploratory Data Analysis Report

## Objective

The objective of this analysis was to understand mutual fund characteristics, transaction behavior, NAV trends, and performance metrics using the Bluestock Mutual Fund Data Warehouse.

## Key Findings

### Fund Categories

Equity-oriented categories dominate the mutual fund landscape, reflecting investor preference for long-term capital appreciation.

### Fund Houses

A small number of AMC providers account for a large share of mutual fund schemes.

### NAV Analysis

NAV values show significant variation across schemes, indicating differences in maturity, investment style, and asset allocation.

### Transaction Analysis

A limited number of states contribute a disproportionately large volume of investor transactions.

### Performance Analysis

Funds with higher Sharpe Ratios generally demonstrate stronger risk-adjusted performance.

### Correlation Analysis

Long-term returns show positive relationships with Sharpe Ratio and Alpha, while Beta demonstrates weaker relationships.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus marketing efforts on high-transaction states.
2. Highlight high-Sharpe funds in investor communication.
3. Expand offerings in dominant fund categories.
4. Develop risk-adjusted performance dashboards for advisors.

## Conclusion

The exploratory analysis provides a strong foundation for performance analytics, dashboard development, and advanced recommendation systems.


## Strategic Findings

1. Equity categories dominate investor preference.
2. Fund distribution is concentrated among a few AMCs.
3. Investment volume is concentrated in a small number of states.
4. High Sharpe Ratio funds consistently demonstrate superior risk-adjusted returns.
5. Several funds generate positive alpha and outperform benchmarks.
6. A subset of funds delivers strong returns without excessive beta exposure.
7. Opportunity exists to recommend high-Sharpe, low-Beta funds to conservative investors.
