# Performance Analytics Report

## Bluestock Mutual Fund Capstone Project

---

# 1. Executive Summary

This report evaluates the performance of mutual fund schemes using industry-standard financial metrics derived from historical Net Asset Value (NAV) data.

The analysis focuses on measuring:

* Growth potential
* Investment risk
* Risk-adjusted returns
* Market sensitivity
* Fund manager performance
* Downside risk

The objective is to identify high-performing mutual funds and generate actionable insights that support portfolio construction and investment decision-making.

---

# 2. Dataset Overview

The performance metrics dataset was generated using cleaned historical NAV data for multiple mutual fund schemes.

### Metrics Calculated

| Metric       | Description                             |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| CAGR         | Compound Annual Growth Rate             |
| Volatility   | Standard deviation of returns           |
| Sharpe Ratio | Risk-adjusted return                    |
| Beta         | Market sensitivity                      |
| Alpha        | Excess return beyond market expectation |
| VaR          | Value at Risk (95% confidence level)    |

The final dataset contains calculated metrics for all mutual fund schemes available in the project.

---

# 3. CAGR Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate long-term growth performance across mutual fund schemes.

## Findings

CAGR measures the annualized growth rate of an investment while accounting for compounding effects.

Funds with the highest CAGR demonstrated superior wealth creation capability over the evaluation period.

### Key Insight

High CAGR funds are attractive for investors seeking long-term capital appreciation.

### Business Recommendation

Growth-oriented investors should prioritize funds exhibiting consistently high CAGR values.

---

# 4. Volatility Analysis

## Objective

Assess investment risk by measuring return variability.

## Findings

Volatility quantifies the fluctuation of returns over time.

Funds with higher volatility experienced larger price movements and greater uncertainty.

### Key Insight

Higher returns are often associated with increased volatility and investment risk.

### Business Recommendation

Risk tolerance should be considered when recommending high-volatility funds to investors.

---

# 5. Sharpe Ratio Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate risk-adjusted performance.

## Findings

Sharpe Ratio measures the amount of return generated per unit of risk.

Funds with high Sharpe Ratios delivered superior risk-adjusted returns.

### Key Insight

A high Sharpe Ratio indicates efficient utilization of risk.

### Business Recommendation

Funds with strong Sharpe Ratios should be prioritized in portfolio recommendation systems.

---

# 6. Alpha Analysis

## Objective

Measure excess returns generated by active fund management.

## Findings

Alpha represents performance above or below expected market returns.

Positive Alpha indicates that a fund outperformed market expectations.

### Key Insight

Funds with positive Alpha demonstrate evidence of successful active management.

### Business Recommendation

Positive Alpha funds can be promoted as actively managed investment opportunities.

---

# 7. Beta Analysis

## Objective

Measure market sensitivity.

## Findings

Beta quantifies how strongly a fund reacts to overall market movements.

### Interpretation

* Beta > 1 → Aggressive Fund
* Beta = 1 → Market-Like Behavior
* Beta < 1 → Defensive Fund

### Key Insight

Higher Beta funds may generate stronger gains during bull markets but also experience larger declines during market downturns.

### Business Recommendation

Beta should be considered when matching investments to investor risk profiles.

---

# 8. Value at Risk (VaR) Analysis

## Objective

Estimate potential downside losses.

## Findings

Value at Risk (VaR) estimates the maximum expected loss at a specified confidence level.

This project uses a 95% confidence interval.

### Key Insight

Funds with larger negative VaR values expose investors to greater downside risk.

### Business Recommendation

VaR should be incorporated into risk management and portfolio allocation decisions.

---

# 9. Risk vs Return Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate the relationship between growth and risk.

## Findings

Risk-return analysis compares CAGR and Volatility across all mutual fund schemes.

The most attractive investments generally exhibit:

* High CAGR
* Low Volatility

### Key Insight

Funds positioned in the high-return, low-risk region provide superior investment efficiency.

### Business Recommendation

Risk-return analysis should be used as a primary screening tool during portfolio construction.

---

# 10. Correlation Analysis

## Objective

Understand relationships among financial performance metrics.

## Findings

Correlation analysis reveals how metrics influence one another.

Examples include:

* Relationship between CAGR and Sharpe Ratio
* Relationship between Volatility and VaR
* Relationship between Alpha and Sharpe Ratio

### Key Insight

Understanding metric interactions improves diversification and risk management strategies.

### Business Recommendation

Portfolio managers should consider metric relationships when selecting complementary investments.

---

# 11. Top Recommended Funds

## Selection Criteria

Funds were ranked using:

1. Sharpe Ratio
2. CAGR
3. Volatility
4. Alpha

## Findings

The highest-ranked funds consistently demonstrated:

* Strong long-term growth
* Attractive risk-adjusted returns
* Positive Alpha
* Acceptable risk levels

### Business Recommendation

These schemes should be considered for inclusion in model portfolios and recommendation engines.

---

# 12. Key Findings

1. Several mutual funds demonstrated strong long-term growth through high CAGR values.

2. Risk levels varied significantly across schemes based on volatility measurements.

3. Sharpe Ratio proved effective in identifying efficient risk-adjusted performers.

4. Positive Alpha funds showed evidence of successful active management.

5. Beta analysis revealed differences in market sensitivity among schemes.

6. VaR highlighted meaningful differences in downside risk exposure.

7. Risk-return analysis identified a subset of highly efficient investment opportunities.

---

# 13. Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

Prioritize funds with strong Sharpe Ratios and high CAGR values.

### Recommendation 2

Use volatility and VaR metrics to align investments with investor risk tolerance.

### Recommendation 3

Incorporate Alpha when evaluating active fund management effectiveness.

### Recommendation 4

Use Beta to classify funds into aggressive and defensive investment categories.

### Recommendation 5

Develop recommendation engines using a combination of growth, risk, and risk-adjusted performance metrics.
